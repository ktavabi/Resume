\begin{cvletter}
I am writing to express my strong interest in the Data Scientist 2 position in the Data Sciences \& Biostatistics group at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. With a Ph.D. in Neuroscience and 15+ years of experience developing statistical and machine learning solutions for complex biological systems, I am excited to contribute to PNNL's mission of scientific discovery, energy resiliency, and national security through advanced data science and biostatistical methods.

\textbf{Biostatistics and Statistical Analysis Expertise}

My background is particularly well-aligned with the Data Sciences \& Biostatistics focus of this role. Throughout my career, I have applied sophisticated biostatistical methods to complex biological datasets, including linear mixed models, longitudinal analysis, case-control designs, and ROC analysis for clinical biomarker development. At The Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, I developed statistical classification systems using nonparametric linear mixed modeling to identify neural biomarkers for language impairment in autism spectrum disorders, achieving diagnostic sensitivity and specificity suitable for clinical translation. This work, which earned me an NIH Loan Repayment Award, demonstrates my ability to apply advanced biostatistical methods to solve real-world biomedical challenges—a skill set directly relevant to PNNL's research mission.

\textbf{Production Data Science and Machine Learning Systems}

As Principal Data Scientist and Business Intelligence Manager at Washington State Department of Corrections, I lead a team of five analysts and have built production-ready ML systems that process millions of records. I developed a production ML pipeline using TF-IDF vectorization and logistic classification to analyze behavioral incident records for 30,000+ individuals, establishing the agency's first ML-driven operational surveillance system. This experience demonstrates my ability to design, deploy, and maintain scalable data science infrastructure—essential capabilities for supporting PNNL's diverse research programs. My expertise in Python (NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, SciPy), R (ggplot2, lme4, Tidyverse), SQL, and cloud computing platforms positions me to immediately contribute to your team's analytical workflows.

\textbf{High-Dimensional Biological Data Analysis}

My 12+ years at the University of Washington's Institute for Learning \& Brain Sciences have equipped me with deep expertise in processing complex, high-dimensional biological datasets. I developed analytical procedures for digital signal processing and data harmonization to process neurophysiological datasets from 500+ participants across multiple research projects. By applying machine learning techniques including Random Forests, logistic regression, PCA, and time-frequency analysis, I consistently extracted meaningful biological insights from multimodal data—including electrophysiological, behavioral, and clinical measurements. This experience with complex biological systems data directly translates to the diverse research challenges PNNL addresses across energy, environmental, and national security domains.

\textbf{Open Science and Reproducible Research}

I have contributed to internationally adopted open-source tools that exemplify best practices in reproducible research. As a contributor to MNE-BIDS and the MNE-Python ecosystem, I helped develop standardized workflows for biological data analysis used by research teams worldwide. At WA State DOC, I established documentation standards and code review processes ensuring consistency and reproducibility across analytical projects, and led initiatives creating open-source analytics publications following established frameworks. This commitment to open science and reproducible research aligns with PNNL's values and ensures that analytical work can be validated, shared, and built upon by the broader scientific community.

\textbf{Cross-Functional Collaboration and Research Leadership}

My experience spans both academic research and applied government settings, demonstrating adaptability across diverse organizational contexts. I have managed over \$2 million in research grants and led interdisciplinary teams of biostatisticians, clinicians, and engineers to translate research into practical applications. In my current role, I provide expert analytical consultation to executive leadership and legislative staff, translating complex analytics into actionable insights for policy and budgetary decisions. This experience in cross-functional collaboration and stakeholder communication is essential for working effectively within PNNL's collaborative research environment.

\textbf{Alignment with PNNL's Mission}

PNNL's commitment to scientific discovery, energy resiliency, and national security resonates deeply with my professional values. My background in statistical modeling of complex systems, combined with experience in both basic research and applied problem-solving, positions me to contribute across PNNL's diverse research portfolio. Whether analyzing environmental data, developing predictive models for energy systems, or applying biostatistical methods to health and biological research, I am eager to apply my skills to advance PNNL's mission.

I am enthusiastic about the opportunity to bring my biostatistics expertise, production data science experience, and collaborative research background to PNNL's Data Sciences \& Biostatistics group. My combination of advanced statistical methods, machine learning capabilities, and proven ability to translate research into actionable insights enables me to make immediate contributions to your team's important work.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to discussing how my experience can support PNNL's innovative research programs.
