  I am excited to apply for the Senior Manager — Biomarker Data Scientist position. As a Principal Data Scientist with a Ph.D. in Neuroscience and over 15 years of experience, I have built a career transforming high-dimensional biological and clinical data into actionable insights and meaningful biomarkers. My expertise spans computational and statistical method development, biomarker analysis in clinical and real-world settings, and advanced modeling with classical machine learning and deep learning. I have a strong record of collaborating across disciplines to translate complex data into impactful solutions, aligning closely with your team’s mission to accelerate global drug development.


  \textbf{Biomarker development and high-dimensional data.} At The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia, I developed and validated neural biomarkers for language impairment in pediatric autism, using machine learning (ROC analysis, classification algorithms) on clinical neurophysiological data. This work enabled diagnostic-level sensitivity and specificity suitable for clinical use. I also built robust statistical analysis pipelines for complex biological signals, earning NIH recognition for translational research. At the University of Washington, I applied advanced ML methods (Random Forest, neural networks, dimensionality reduction) to high-dimensional, multimodal datasets—neurophysiological, behavioral, and clinical—in longitudinal pediatric studies. I designed scalable, Python-based workflows and contributed to widely adopted open-source tools (MNE-Python, MNE-BIDS), supporting global research teams. My experience shows hands-on expertise in formulating, testing, and validating predictive models for challenging, high-dimensional, and longitudinal data.

  \textbf{Real-world data, scale, and collaboration.} In my current role at the Washington State Department of Corrections, I lead a team of business analysts to architect data science infrastructure (Python, R, SQL, cloud) for longitudinal and real-world datasets. I have deployed production ML pipelines that process data at scale, delivering high-throughput classification and evidence-based decision support for over 27,000 cases annually. My approach combines technical rigor with effective team leadership, clear communication, and a commitment to quality deliverables, even amid competing priorities. I thrive in cross-functional environments, working closely with clinicians, statisticians, and technical teams to translate data into clear, actionable insights. I am excited about the prospect of bringing this blend of biomarker science, advanced analytics, and collaborative problem-solving to BMS to help drive innovation in drug development.

 Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my background and passion for translational science can support the Drug Development Data Science and Biomarker team’s mission.
